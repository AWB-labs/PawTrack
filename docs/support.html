Common questions

How do I invite someone to help care for a pet?

Open the pet's profile, tap Invite a caregiver, choose what they can do and for how long, and share the invite link or code. You can review or revoke their access at any time from the pet's Caregivers screen.

How do I turn off or change reminders?

Go to Settings → Reminders to choose which reminders you get, and to set quiet hours.

How do I delete a pet's record?

Open the pet's profile, scroll to Manage, and choose Delete. This removes the pet and everything logged for it — for you and everyone you've shared it with — and can't be undone.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Profile and account and choose to delete your account. If you run into any trouble, email us and we'll take care of it.

I found a bug, or the app crashed

Email us a quick description of what happened and, if you can, what you were doing right before it — screenshots help a lot. We read every report.
