Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Petal ("Petal", "we", "us") is operated by AWB Labs. This policy explains what information Petal collects when you use the app, why we collect it, who can see it, and the choices you have. By using Petal, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you create a Petal account, we collect your email address, a display name, and (optionally) a profile photo. Authentication is handled by our backend provider (Supabase); if you sign in with Apple, we receive only the name and email Apple chooses to share.

Pet information you add

Anything you enter about a pet stays tied to that pet's record, including: name, species, breed, birthday or approximate age, photos, allergies and conditions, weight history, vaccination records, vet-visit write-ups (reason, diagnosis, treatment, cost, clinic and vet name), feeding schedules, and medicine schedules and dose logs.

Caregiver & sharing information

If you invite someone to help care for a pet, we store the invite, the specific permissions you grant them, and the time window those permissions are active. If you accept someone else's invite, the same applies to your membership on their pet.

Community content

If you choose to post to Petal's community feed, that post — and that it's attributed to you — is visible to other people you're connected with through shared pets.

Device & notification data

We collect a push-notification token so we can deliver reminders you've turned on (meals, doses, vet follow-ups). App preferences — theme, weight units, notification settings — are stored locally on your device.

Biometric app-lock

If your device supports Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint unlock, Petal can optionally use it to lock the app. That match is performed entirely by your device's operating system — Petal never receives, transmits, or stores your face or fingerprint data, only a yes/no result reported by the device itself.

Diagnostic information

If something goes wrong, we may collect basic error and crash information to help us fix it. This does not include the contents of your pets' records.

We don't collect: payment or financial information (Petal doesn't currently process payments), precise location, or contacts from your device.

2. How we use this information

  • To provide Petal's core features and keep your data in sync across your devices
  • To enable the caregiver sharing you set up, exactly as you configure it
  • To send reminders and notifications you've enabled
  • To diagnose bugs and keep the app reliable
  • To respond when you contact support

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or ad targeting.

3. Who can see your data

By default, only you can see a pet's records. When you invite a caregiver, they can see and act on exactly what you've granted them — nothing more — and only for as long as the access window you set is open. You can review or revoke anyone's access at any time from inside the app.

Beyond that, your data is shared only with the service providers below, and only as needed to run the app — never for their own marketing purposes.

5. Data retention & deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You're in control of it:

  • You can delete an individual pet's record at any time from within the app — this permanently removes that pet and everything logged for it.
  • You can delete your account entirely from Settings → Account. This permanently removes your account and personal data from our systems.

If you need help with either, contact us at aliezz140@gmail.com.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to withdraw consent for us to process it. You can exercise most of these directly in the app; for anything else, contact us and we'll help.

7. Children's privacy

Petal is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we'll remove it.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit between the app and our servers. Access to pet records is enforced at the database level, so a caregiver can only ever read or write what you've explicitly granted them — no one, including us, bypasses that by default.

9. International data transfers

Because we rely on service providers that operate internationally, your information may be processed in a country other than the one you live in.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, where appropriate, let you know in the app.

11. Contact us
