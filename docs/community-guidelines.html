Community Rules

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Petal has a community feed — a place to show off a dog who has just discovered snow, ask whether anyone else's cat does that, and find people who look after the same kind of animal you do. These are the rules for it, in plain English. The binding version is section 5 of the Terms of Use.

Zero tolerance. Objectionable content and abusive behaviour have no place here and no warning tier. If you post it, it comes down and your account goes with it.

What isn't allowed

None of the following is permitted anywhere in the app — in posts, comments, photos, your display name, your bio, or a pet's profile:

  • Harassment and bullying. Going after a person, piling on, or repeatedly contacting someone who doesn't want to hear from you.
  • Hate speech. Slurs, or attacking anyone for who they are — their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, age, sex, gender identity, or sexual orientation.
  • Sexual content. Nudity, sexual content, or soliciting either. This is a pet app.
  • Anything involving a minor sexually. Reported to the authorities as well as removed.
  • Animal cruelty. Showing, encouraging, instructing in, or celebrating harm to an animal — including organised animal fighting. This is the community's founding rule.
  • Violence and threats. Threatening a person, or wishing harm on them.
  • Promotion of self-harm. If you are struggling, please talk to someone you trust or a crisis line where you live — a feed can't help the way a person can.
  • Impersonation. Pretending to be someone else, or passing off a pet or an affiliation that isn't yours.
  • Spam and scams. Bulk promotion, selling, or trying to take somebody in.
  • Anything illegal, or that infringes someone else's rights.

How to report something

Every post and every comment has a report control — the ⋯ button on a post, and Report under a comment. Tap it, pick the closest reason, and add anything you think we should know.

  • The content leaves your feed the moment you report it. You don't have to look at it while we work.
  • Serious categories — hate speech, sexual content, threats, animal cruelty, self-harm — are hidden from everyone automatically on the first report, before a person sees it.
  • Reports are private. The person you report is never told who reported them.
  • You can see everything you've reported, and where it got to, in Settings → Safety.

How to block someone

From the same ⋯ control, choose Block.

  • Blocking is immediate and works both ways — neither of you sees the other's posts or comments anywhere in the app.
  • Blocking from a post or comment also sends that content to our safety team, so we know why.
  • You never have to explain a block, and you can undo it any time in Settings → Safety.

What we do about it

We review every report within 24 hours. When we find that the rules have been broken, we remove the content and eject the account that posted it — the account is terminated, and everything it has posted comes down with it. When a report turns out to be mistaken, we put the content back.

Creating a new account to get around a block or a suspension is itself a violation, and ends the new account too.

Filtering

Before anything is published, Petal checks the text against a list of objectionable terms and refuses to post a match. It is a floor, not a ceiling — no word list catches cruelty phrased politely, and every list occasionally objects to something innocent. That is exactly why the report control exists, and why a report from a person outranks a filter.

Your pets' records are not part of this

Nothing in your pets' health records — weights, doses, vaccinations, vet visits, documents — is ever in the community feed. Only what you deliberately post is public, and you can take any of it down at any time.
